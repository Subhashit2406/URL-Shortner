# Module 01 Quick Pass: SkillSwap Requirements

## 5 Explicit Requirements
1. Learners can browse providers by category and view provider profiles with ratings.
2. Learners can book time slots and pay through the platform.
3. Providers can set their own availability, pricing, and service descriptions.
4. The platform charges a 15% commission on provider earnings.
5. The system must support expansion from 1 city to 5 cities within 6 months.

## 2 Key Ambiguities
1. How are booking time slot conflicts and cancellation refunds handled between learners and providers?
2. What are the exact performance latency metrics for search feeling "instant"?

## 1 Critical Question for PM
- "Does the 15% platform commission apply uniformly to all service categories, or are payouts structured differently based on session duration and provider tier?"
