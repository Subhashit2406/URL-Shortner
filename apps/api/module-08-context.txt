Why This Matters In Production (2 Minutes)

Search endpoints often become the top source of outages because they combine:

- unbounded queries (expensive)
- user-controlled inputs (security risk)
- lots of edge cases (correctness)

The goal is not “fancy search”. ‌‌‌‌‌​‌‌​‌‌​​​‌​‌‌‌‌‌​The goal is “safe and predictable search”.

‌​‌​​​‌​‌​​​‌‌‌​‌​‌​‌‌Real incident (30 seconds):

- An unbounded search query triggered full table scans during peak traffic and took down the primary database. ​​‌​​​‌​​​‌​​​‌​‌​​‌‌​“Just add search” turned into an outage.
